1. About the Game

Emoji Match: Association is a picture puzzle about connections. Every board is a grid of illustrated cards, and every row of four hides one idea. Find the four cards that belong together, gather them into one row and the row clears.

2. How to Play

  1. Drag a card onto another and the two swap places
  2. Line up four cards that share a theme and the row clears, showing its category name
  3. Watch your moves: clear every row before the move counter reaches zero

3. Frequently Asked Questions

I'm stuck. What can help?

The buttons under the board are boosters. The lightbulb gives a hint, the magnifier reveals a category, and unlimited moves lets you play without counting for a while. Later on an extra row gives you more room to sort cards. When a booster has no uses left, a short optional video gives you more.

I ran out of moves. Is the level lost?

No. You can watch a short optional video for 20 more moves, or close the pop-up to start the same level again from the beginning.

Does the game have in-app purchases?

No. The game is free and supported by advertising. It never asks you for payment details.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device, automatically, after every level you finish. It is not stored online, so uninstalling the game or moving to a new phone starts again from the first level.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Tap the gear icon in the top right of the board to open Settings. Sound and vibration both live there.

4. Troubleshooting

  1. Close the game fully and reopen it
  2. Check that you have the latest version installed
  3. Restart your device
  4. If the problem continues, email us with your device model and a short description of the issue
