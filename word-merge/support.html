1. About the Game

Kelime Birleştir (Word Merge) is a word-association puzzle game. The board is full of word bubbles: drag related words onto each other to merge them, complete every group before you run out of moves, and clear the level.

2. How to Play

  1. Look at the bubbles: each one holds a word
  2. Drag a word onto a related word: if they belong together, they merge
  3. Complete a group: when every word of a category is merged, the bubble pops
  4. Pop all groups before your moves run out to win the level

3. Frequently Asked Questions

I'm stuck. Is there a hint?

Yes. Three helpers unlock as you play and sit in the row under the board: the Magnet pulls a matching pair together, the Hint points at two words that go together, and Category Reveal names one of the hidden categories. You get one of each free on the level it unlocks in. After that they are bought with coins.

I ran out of moves. Have I lost the level?

Not yet. When the moves run out the game offers you more: watch a short video for a few extra moves, or pay coins for a bigger top-up. Closing that card is what ends the level, and you can always play it again.

Why can't I start a level?

Starting a level costs a heart, and you can hold five. A heart comes back by itself every half hour, and a level you win gives its heart straight back. If you are out of hearts you can wait for one, or refill with coins.

Does the game have in-app purchases?

Yes. Word Merge is free to play and supported by ads, and everything in it can be reached by playing. If you would rather not wait or watch, you can buy coin packs, or remove the ads for good. Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play, so we never see your card details.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device automatically. If you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), progress can also sync across your devices.

How do I change the language or turn off sound?

Tap the gear icon on the top right to open Settings; sound, haptics and language options live there.

4. Troubleshooting

  1. Close the game fully and reopen it
  2. Check that you have the latest version installed
  3. Restart your device
  4. If the problem continues, email us with your device model and a short description of the issue
