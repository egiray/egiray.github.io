1. About the Game

Kelime Birleştir (Word Merge) is a word-association puzzle game. The board is full of word bubbles — drag related words onto each other to merge them, complete every group before you run out of moves, and clear the level.

2. How to Play

  1. Look at the bubbles — each one holds a word
  2. Drag a word onto a related word — if they belong together, they merge
  3. Complete a group — when every word of a category is merged, the bubble pops
  4. Pop all groups before your moves run out to win the level

3. Frequently Asked Questions

I'm stuck — is there a hint?

Yes. Tap the lightbulb button on the left of the top bar. The badge on it shows how many hints you have left; hints carry over between levels, and you get free ones back over time. If you run out, you can watch a short video for one more or trade in coins for it.

Does the game have in-app purchases?

No. Word Merge is completely free and is supported by ads — there is nothing to buy, and the game never asks for payment details. Coins are earned by playing or by watching optional videos.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device automatically. If you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), progress can also sync across your devices.

How do I change the language or turn off sound?

Tap the gear icon on the top right to open Settings — sound, haptics and language options live there.

4. Troubleshooting

  1. Close the game fully and reopen it
  2. Check that you have the latest version installed
  3. Restart your device
  4. If the problem continues, email us with your device model and a short description of the issue
