Color Sort: Jelly Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Summary: Color Sort: Jelly Puzzle never asks you for personal information. There is no account with us and no form to fill in, so we never learn your name, your email address or your exact location. We use trusted third-party services: Google Firebase for analytics and remote settings, Google AdMob for advertising (with Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize bidding through it), AppsFlyer for install attribution and Sentry for crash reports. In-app purchases of coins and bundles are processed by Apple or Google and managed through RevenueCat. Cloud save is optional.

1. Overview

Color Sort: Jelly Puzzle ("the App") is a mobile color sorting puzzle game developed by Erdem Giray. This privacy policy explains how we and our third-party partners handle information in the App.

The App is supported by advertising and offers in-app purchases of coins and bundles. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details, and the App never asks you for them.

2. Information We Collect

The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number or address, and it does not require an account. There is no login of our own, no contact form and no chat in the game, and nothing you write or create is sent to us. The information below is handled by the third-party services in section 3:

Data Type Purpose Service
App usage events (analytics) Analytics, App Functionality Firebase Analytics
Crash logs and diagnostics App Functionality (fixing crashes) Sentry
Device and Advertising IDs (shared with partners) Advertising, Analytics, Attribution Google AdMob (with Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize), Firebase, AppsFlyer
Purchase records Delivering and Restoring Purchases Apple App Store / Google Play, RevenueCat
Saved game progress (cloud save, optional) Cloud Save Apple Game Center (iCloud) / Google Play Games
Name, email address, phone number, address, precise location, contacts, messages, calendar, photos, files, audio, health data, chat or user content Not collected at all None

Crash reports are used only by us to fix the App. Purchase records and cloud save are handled by Apple, Google and RevenueCat, as described in section 3.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Firebase (Analytics and Remote Config)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players use the App. We also use Firebase Remote Config to change a few game settings without a new release.

Data collected:

  • App usage events, such as how the game is played and which features are used
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • General location (country or region, not precise location)
  • An app instance identifier
  • App performance data

Privacy Policy: Firebase Privacy Policy

Google AdMob

We display banner, interstitial (full-screen) and rewarded video advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect data to show relevant ads. Where the law requires it (the EEA and the UK), Google's consent form (its User Messaging Platform) is shown before any ad is requested, and your answer decides whether the ads you see are personalized. You can reopen this form from Settings in the game. On iOS, Apple also asks whether you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, the advertising identifier is not used for tracking.

Rewarded videos are your choice. You can watch one to get an extra life. Nothing in the game forces you to watch one.

Data collected:

  • Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data

AdMob runs an auction for each ad slot, and other advertising networks bid in it through AdMob's mediation. Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize take part that way, so they can receive the same advertising identifier and device information.

Opt-out: You can limit ad tracking in your device settings. On iOS, turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track". On Android, opt out of ads personalization in your Google settings.

Privacy Policies: Google Privacy Policy · Unity Privacy Policy · Liftoff Privacy Policy

AppsFlyer (Attribution)

AppsFlyer tells us which advertisement a player arrived from, so that we know whether advertising the game was worth paying for. It counts the install and the sessions.

Data collected:

  • A device identifier, and the advertising identifier
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Install and session counts

On iOS: The tracking prompt described under AdMob applies here too. If you say no, the advertising identifier is not used for tracking.

Privacy Policy: AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy

Sentry (Crash Reports)

When the App crashes or hits an error, Sentry sends us a report so that we can find and fix the problem. A report describes the error, not you.

Data collected:

  • The error and where in the App it happened
  • Device information (model, OS version) and the App version
  • IP address, used by Sentry to receive the report

Privacy Policy: Sentry Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases (Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat)

The App sells coins and bundles. Each purchase is paid through the App Store or Google Play, and we never see your card, your billing address or your store account.

We use RevenueCat to check which purchases belong to this device, so that Restore Purchases in Settings can bring them back after a reinstall or on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous random identifier generated for your installation, not your name or your email address. What it handles is the purchase record, not your payment details.

Data collected:

  • The store receipt (the purchase record) that proves the purchase
  • An anonymous random identifier generated for your installation
  • Device and app information

Privacy Policies: Apple Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy · RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Cloud Save (Apple Game Center / Google Play Games)

Cloud save is optional. On iPhone it uses Apple Game Center, on Android Google Play Games. When it is on, a copy of your saved game progress is kept in your own Game Center (iCloud) or Google Play Games account, so you can continue after a reinstall or on another device.

The copy is stored by Apple or Google under their own policies. We cannot read it, and your Game Center or Google Play Games identity is handled by Apple or Google. We never learn your name or your email address.

You sign in from the account row in Settings. On iPhone, Apple's own sign in sheet appears. You can decline, and the game plays on as normal. You can switch cloud save off in Settings: that stops uploads and leaves the copy already in your account, which you can delete there.

Privacy Policies: Apple Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy

4. Local Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Game progress: the level you are on, your coins and boosters, and which tutorials you have seen
  • Settings preferences, such as sound, music, haptics (vibration) and notifications

Apart from the optional cloud save described above, which keeps a copy of your progress in your own Game Center or Google Play Games account while it is switched on, this data stays on your device. You can delete it by uninstalling the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and in fact the App collects no personal information from anyone. The third-party services we use (Firebase, AdMob and the networks bidding through it, AppsFlyer, Sentry, RevenueCat) have their own policies regarding children's data.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Data Retention

  • Local data: Retained until you uninstall the App
  • Analytics data: Retained by Firebase according to their data retention policies (typically 14 months)
  • Attribution data: Retained by AppsFlyer according to its own data retention policy
  • Crash reports: Retained by Sentry according to its own data retention policy
  • Ad data: Managed by Google, Unity and Liftoff according to their privacy policies
  • Purchase records: Kept by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat for as long as they are needed to restore the purchase
  • Cloud save: Kept in your own Game Center or Google Play Games account until you delete it there

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request information about data collected
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the App removes the progress stored on your device. Cloud save lives in your own Game Center or Google Play Games account, and you can delete it there at any time. You can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google and RevenueCat for as long as they are needed to restore the purchase.
  • Opt-out: Disable ad tracking and analytics via device settings. In the EEA and the UK you can also change your ad consent choice from Settings in the game.

To exercise these rights regarding third-party services, please refer to their respective privacy policies linked above. If you would like to ask us about any of this, write to the address at the end of this page.
