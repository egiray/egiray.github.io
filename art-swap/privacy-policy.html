Art Swap - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Summary: Art Swap never asks you for personal information. There is no account, no sign in and no form to fill in, so we never learn your name, your email address or where you are. We use trusted third-party services for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, attribution, the one purchase and optional cloud save. The game is free and supported by advertising, and it has a single optional purchase that removes the automatic ads.

1. Overview

Art Swap: Picture Puzzle Game ("the App") is a mobile picture puzzle game developed by Erdem Giray. This privacy policy explains how we and our third-party partners handle information in our App.

The App is free and supported by advertising. It contains one in-app purchase, "Remove Ads", which switches the automatic ads off for good. That purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store: we never see your payment details, and the App never asks you for them.

2. Information We Collect

The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number or address, and it does not require an account. There is no login and no contact form anywhere in the game, so there is no point at which you could hand us personal information even if you wanted to. Everything below is collected automatically by the third-party services in section 3:

Data Type Purpose Service
In-App Actions (usage data) Analytics, App Functionality Firebase Analytics
Device and Advertising IDs (shared with partners) Advertising, Analytics Google AdMob, Firebase, AppsFlyer
Crash Logs and Diagnostics Bug Fixing Sentry
Purchase History App Functionality Google Play Billing / Apple App Store, RevenueCat
Game Progress Cloud Save (optional) Google Play Games / Apple Game Center
Name, email address, phone number, address, location, contacts, messages, calendar, photos, files, audio, health data Not collected at all None

Of the rows above, only the device and advertising identifiers are shared with anyone, and only for advertising and analytics. Everything the App sends travels over an encrypted connection.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Firebase (Analytics & Remote Config)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players use our App, and Firebase Remote Config to change a few game values without shipping a new version.

Data collected:

  • App usage events: puzzle starts and finishes, the tutorial, hints, restarts, skips, ad starts and ends, settings changes, the rating ask and the two offers
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • General location (country or region, not precise location)
  • An app instance identifier
  • App performance data

Privacy Policy: Firebase Privacy Policy

Google AdMob

We display banner, interstitial and rewarded video advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect data to show relevant ads. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and your answer decides whether the ads you see are personalized. On iOS, ads are only personalized if you also allow tracking when the system asks.

The rewarded videos are always your choice: one doubles a gift of hints, one replays a past daily puzzle, and one hands you a hint when your stock is empty. Nothing in the game forces you to watch one.

Data collected:

  • Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data

AdMob runs an auction for each ad slot, and other advertising networks bid in it through AdMob's mediation. Liftoff Monetize takes part that way, so it can receive the same advertising identifier and device information.

Opt-out: You can limit ad tracking in your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking > disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

Privacy Policies: Google Privacy Policy · Liftoff Privacy Policy

AppsFlyer (Attribution)

AppsFlyer tells us which advertisement a player arrived from, so that we know whether advertising the game was worth paying for. It counts the install and the session, and it is told two things afterwards: that a campaign level was completed, and that the ad removal was bought. It is never told what you paid.

Data collected:

  • A device identifier, and the advertising identifier
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Install and session counts

On iOS: Apple's "Allow this app to track your activity" prompt appears once per install, the first time you reach a board. If you say no, the advertising identifier is not used for tracking.

Privacy Policy: AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy

Sentry

We use Sentry for crash reporting and error tracking to help us identify and fix bugs quickly. The Sentry project for this game is hosted on Sentry's servers in the European Union.

Data collected:

  • Crash logs and error messages
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App state at time of crash

Privacy Policy: Sentry Privacy Policy

The Remove Ads Purchase (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store and RevenueCat)

There is one thing to buy in this game and there will only ever be one: the automatic ads go away, forever, for a single payment. The payment itself is taken by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your card, your billing address or your store account.

We use RevenueCat to check whether this device owns that purchase, so that the ads stay off after a reinstall or on a second device. What it handles is the purchase record, not your payment details.

Privacy Policies: Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy · RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Cloud Save (Apple Game Center / Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), your game progress may be copied to your platform account so you can continue on another device. The copy lives in your own account, stored by Apple or Google under their own policies. We do not operate our own game servers, we hold no player accounts, and the game never reads who you are.

It is optional in the real sense: signed out, offline, or having said no is a complete and fully playable state. Nothing in the game waits for an account and nothing asks you twice.

Privacy Policies: Apple Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy

Puzzle Picture Downloads

The App ships with the first puzzles inside it and fetches the pictures for the rest from our content delivery network as you reach them. Each picture is an ordinary file request over an encrypted connection. It carries no account and no identifier of ours, because there is neither; as with any web request, the server has to see your device's IP address in order to send the file back. A picture you have played stays on your device, and nothing about your play is sent along with the request.

4. Local Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Game progress: the puzzle you are on, your hint stock, the gift bar, and whether you have seen the first-move tutorial
  • Settings preferences: sounds, music, vibrations, dark mode, the congrats pop-up
  • Whether the ads have been removed on this device
  • Puzzle pictures that have been downloaded, so they open offline

Apart from the optional cloud save described above, this data stays on your device. You can delete it by uninstalling the App.

5. Permissions

The App requests minimal permissions:

  • Internet and network state: Required for ads, analytics, the puzzle picture downloads, the purchase and cloud save
  • Vibration: For haptic feedback (can be disabled in settings)
  • Advertising ID: Used by the advertising and analytics services described above

The App asks for no other permission. Saving a finished picture to your photo gallery uses the system's own gallery route and needs no access to your photos: the App writes one picture and can read none of them.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and in fact the App collects no personal information from anyone. The third-party services we use (Firebase, AdMob, AppsFlyer, Sentry, RevenueCat) have their own policies regarding children's data.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention

  • Local data: Retained until you uninstall the App
  • Analytics data: Retained by Firebase according to their data retention policies (typically 14 months)
  • Crash data: Retained by Sentry according to their retention policy (typically 90 days)
  • Ad data: Managed by Google and its mediation partners according to their privacy policies
  • Attribution data: Retained by AppsFlyer according to their retention policy
  • Purchase records: Kept for as long as the purchase is valid, by the store that took the payment and by RevenueCat
  • Cloud save: Kept in your own Game Center or Google Play Games account until you delete it there

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request information about data collected
  • Deletion: We do not run our own game servers and hold no player accounts, so there is very little for us to delete. What you can remove:
    • Data on your device. Uninstalling the App removes it.
    • Cloud save. It lives in your own Game Center or Google Play Games account, and you can delete it there at any time.
    • Your advertising identifier. You can reset it, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.
  • Opt-out: Disable ad tracking and analytics via device settings

To exercise these rights regarding third-party services, please refer to their respective privacy policies linked above. If you would like to ask us about any of this, write to the address at the end of this page.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data processed through our App. Everything the App sends or fetches travels over an encrypted connection. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. Our third-party partners maintain their own security practices.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
