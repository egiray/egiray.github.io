Fox Rambler - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Summary: Fox Rambler never asks you for personal information. There is no account, no sign in and no form to fill in, so we never learn your name, your email address or where you live. We use trusted third-party services for analytics, advertising, install attribution, crash reporting and purchases. The game is free to play and supported by advertising, and it offers optional in-app purchases that are paid for through Apple or Google, so we never see your payment details.

1. Overview

Fox Rambler ("the App") is a mobile action-adventure game developed by Erdem Giray. This privacy policy explains how we and our third-party partners handle information in our App.

The App is free to download and supported by advertising. It also offers optional in-app purchases (gem packs and an ad-free unlock). Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play, which handle payment themselves: we never receive or store your card number or billing address.

2. Information We Collect

The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number or address, and it does not require an account. There is no login and no contact form anywhere in the game. Everything below is collected automatically by the third-party services in section 3:

Data Type Purpose Service
In-App Actions (usage data) Analytics, App Functionality Firebase Analytics
Device and Advertising IDs (shared with partners) Advertising, Analytics, Attribution Google AdMob, Firebase, AppsFlyer
Advertising Data Third-Party Advertising Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize
Install Source Knowing Which Advert Brought an Install AppsFlyer
Crash and Performance Data Bug Fixing Sentry
Purchase History Delivering and Restoring Purchases Apple / Google, RevenueCat

3. Third-Party Services

Google Firebase (Analytics & Remote Config)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players use our App, and Firebase Remote Config to deliver game configuration updates.

Data collected:

  • App usage events (sessions, islands reached, rewarded videos watched, purchases)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • General location (country/region, not precise location)
  • App performance data

Privacy Policy: Firebase Privacy Policy

Google AdMob (with Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize)

The App shows optional rewarded video ads using Google AdMob: you choose to watch a video in exchange for an in-game reward. AdMob may collect data to show relevant ads. On iOS, ads are only personalized if you allow tracking when the system asks; where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), a consent message is shown before personalized ads are served.

Data collected:

  • Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android)
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data

AdMob runs an auction for each ad, and other advertising networks bid in it through AdMob's mediation. Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize take part that way, so they can receive the same advertising identifier and device information.

Opt-out: You can limit ad tracking in your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy > Tracking > disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

Privacy Policies: Google Privacy Policy · Unity Privacy Policy · Liftoff Privacy Policy

AppsFlyer (Attribution)

AppsFlyer tells us which advertisement a player arrived from, so that we know whether advertising the game was worth paying for. It counts the install and the session, and it is told one thing afterwards: that an island was completed, and which one.

Data collected:

  • A device identifier, and the advertising identifier
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Install and session counts

On iOS: Apple's "Allow this app to track your activity" prompt appears once per install. If you say no, the advertising identifier is not used for tracking.

Privacy Policy: AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy

Sentry (Crash Reporting)

If the App crashes, freezes or hits an error, Sentry sends us a report so that we can fix it.

Data collected:

  • Crash and error details
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App version

Privacy Policy: Sentry Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Purchases are paid for through the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm a purchase and to restore it on a new device. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and an anonymous identifier created by the App, never your payment details.

Privacy Policies: Apple Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy · RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Local Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Game progress (islands reached, weapons, gems, coins and other items)
  • Settings preferences (sound, music, vibration)

This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to us. You can delete it by uninstalling the App.

5. Permissions

The App requests minimal permissions:

  • Internet Access: Required for ads, analytics, remote configuration and purchases
  • Vibration: For haptic feedback (can be disabled in settings)
  • Tracking (iOS): Apple's tracking prompt, as described above. The App works the same whichever answer you give.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The third-party services we use have their own policies regarding children's data.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention

  • Local data: Retained until you uninstall the App
  • Analytics data: Retained by Firebase according to their data retention policies (typically 14 months)
  • Crash data: Retained by Sentry for 90 days
  • Ad and attribution data: Managed by Google, Unity, Liftoff and AppsFlyer according to their privacy policies
  • Purchase records: Kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat so that purchases can be restored

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request information about data collected
  • Deletion: We do not run our own servers and hold no player accounts, so there is very little for us to delete. Uninstalling the App removes everything stored on your device.
  • Opt-out: Disable ad tracking and analytics via device settings

To exercise these rights regarding third-party services, please refer to their respective privacy policies linked above.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data processed through our App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. Our third-party partners maintain their own security practices.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
