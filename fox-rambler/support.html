1. About the Game

Fox Rambler is an action-adventure game across a chain of islands. Lead the fox through each island, defeat the creatures in your way, collect loot and chests, grow stronger with better weapons, and take on each island's boss to open the way to the next one.

2. How to Play

  1. Move: drag anywhere on the screen to walk
  2. Fight: walk up to a creature and the fox attacks by itself
  3. Grow stronger: spend what you collect on new and better weapons
  4. Beat the boss of an island to move on to the next

3. Frequently Asked Questions

Does the game have in-app purchases?

Yes. Fox Rambler is free to play and supported by ads, and everything in it can be reached by playing. If you like, you can buy gem packs, or remove the ads for good. Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play, so we never see your card details.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. The only ads are optional videos you choose to watch for a reward. With the ad-free purchase, those rewards are given straight away with no video.

I bought something on another device. How do I get it back?

Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account. Sign in with the same account and they are restored.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device automatically. It is not stored online, so uninstalling the game removes it.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Open Settings from the game screen: it has volume sliders for sound and music, and a vibration switch.

4. Troubleshooting

  1. Close the game fully and reopen it
  2. Check that you have the latest version installed
  3. Restart your device
  4. If the problem continues, email us with your device model and a short description of the issue
